# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- Target selectors `@a @p @r @s @e @n` with all filters in every EssentialsX command, from the console, command blocks and inside `/execute`
- `/tp` with coordinates, relative and local positions, rotation, `facing` and entities, respecting `/execute in`, `at` and `positioned`
- `/kill`, `/clear`, `/enchant`, `/give`, `/item`, `/recipe` and `/list` gained the vanilla functions they lost
- `/xp add`, `set` and `query`, `/time` and `/weather` with units
- Tab completion for the added functions
- Ten languages and a `config.yml` to switch each addition off
